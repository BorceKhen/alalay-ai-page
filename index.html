ALALAY-AI

ALALAY-AI: A Machine Learning-Adaptive Reading Platform for Individuals with Learning and Visual Disabilities

ALALAY-AI is a web-based adaptive reading platform engineered to provide accessible, personalized reading assistance for individuals with dyslexia, total blindness, low vision, and color blindness. Utilizing the Unified Hybrid Text Extraction Model (UHTEM) with 98.43% character recognition accuracy, the platform converts PDF, DOCX, and PPTX materials into simplified study sets, flashcards, and quizzes. Powered by Deep Q-Network (DQN) reinforcement learning for sequential behavioral adaptation and tiered generative AI content synthesis, ALALAY-AI dynamically tailors reading difficulty, line rulers, text-to-speech audio, and visual presentation to individual accessibility profiles.
